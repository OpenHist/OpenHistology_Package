# Third-party notices

OpenHisto’s original code is distributed under the project [MIT license](LICENSE).
Bundled third-party source and adapted routines retain the notices and terms
listed below; the project license does not replace them.

This inventory links recorded provenance and notice files. It does not assign
a license where none is recorded, or infer whole-tree coverage from a license
inside a nested component.

## Pinned MIL source snapshots

The package records **29 source snapshots from 28 upstream repositories**,
supporting 31 native-backed profiles. The remaining three MIL profiles are
original statistical pooling baselines. Full pins and source selections are in
[SOURCE_CATALOG.json](src/openhisto/mil/models/code_bases/SOURCE_CATALOG.json)
and the individual source records linked in the first column.

**10 of the 29 records have no license file recorded.** The other 19 records
list 21 notice files. Four records list only nested-component license files:
`mamba_mil`, `mhim_mil`, `mhim_mil_v1` and `rrt_mil`.

## Separately installed models, dependencies and datasets

Optional libraries, separately obtained model weights, native VLM worker
environments and datasets have their own terms. They are not assigned the
project MIT license by this inventory. Access and terms references are retained
in the [encoder registry](src/openhisto/feature_encoder/registry.py),
[VLM registry](src/openhisto/vlas/registry.py),
[segmentation guide](src/openhisto/segmentation/README.md), and
[dataset guide](src/openhisto/default_datasets/README.md).
The source-only MIL snapshots do not bundle pretrained weights or patient data.
